Our Services

Explore the range of smart tools designed to make agriculture sustainable, efficient, and profitable.

Personalized Crop Recommendation

Enter your local district, season, soil type, and water availability, and our system filters and recommends the most suitable crops. Growing tips and yield data are provided for each crop.

Weather Bulletins & Advisories

Get weather conditions including temperature ranges, humidity values, wind velocity, and sunrise/sunset alerts. Each condition is coupled with clear farming recommendations.

Fertilizer Calculator & Application Guides

Select your crop, soil composition, and plant growth stage. The system returns exact quantity calculations, recommended application methods (broadcasting, fertigation), and critical safety tips.

Pest & Disease Diagnosis Database

Review common pests and crop diseases. Learn to identify issues early by reading detailed lists of symptoms, biological causes, preventative methods, and recommended chemical/organic treatment agents.

Irrigation Optimization Advices

Understand the water requirement metrics for your crops. Access customized irrigation scheduling advice and water-saving tips (e.g. Alternate Wetting and Drying, mulching, drip installations).

Market Price Monitoring (APMC Mandis)

Access daily commodity prices from markets across Karnataka. Real-time updates help you know when and where to sell your harvest, ensuring you get the best economic return.
